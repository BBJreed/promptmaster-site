AI image prompt examples: five rough prompts, before and after

Image models fill every gap with a guess: the style, the light, the angle. A good image prompt names those things so the first result is close to what you pictured.

Updated October 27, 2026

The six parts of an image prompt

  1. Subject. What is in the picture, specifically. "An orange tabby cat", not "a cat".
  2. Setting. Where and when. "On a rainy Tokyo street at night."
  3. Style. Photo, watercolor, 3D render, flat illustration, pencil sketch.
  4. Lighting. Golden hour, neon, soft studio light, overcast.
  5. Camera. Close-up, wide shot, low angle, shallow depth of field.
  6. Negative prompt. What to leave out: text, watermark, blur, extra fingers.

1. A pet photo

Before: A cat in a city.

After: An orange tabby cat sitting on a rainy Tokyo street at night, neon signs reflected in puddles, realistic photo, low angle, shallow depth of field, cinematic lighting. Negative prompt: blurry, extra limbs, text, watermark.

2. A product shot

Before: My coffee mug for an ad.

After: A matte white ceramic coffee mug with steam rising, on a light oak table beside an open notebook, soft morning window light from the left, clean product photo, 45-degree angle, plenty of empty space on the right for text. Negative prompt: logos, clutter, harsh shadows.

3. A book or blog illustration

Before: Picture for my blog about saving money.

After: A flat vector illustration of a young woman dropping coins into a glass jar labeled "Trip", warm pastel colors, simple shapes, plain light background, wide 16:9 format. Negative prompt: realistic photo, text other than the label, dark colors.

4. A portrait

Before: A wizard.

After: Portrait of an elderly wizard with a long silver beard and a deep blue hooded robe, holding a glowing crystal, digital painting in a classic fantasy style, warm candlelight from below, close-up from the chest up, dark stone background. Negative prompt: modern clothing, text, extra hands.

5. A logo idea

Before: Logo for my bakery.

After: A simple flat logo icon for a small bakery called [name]: a rolling pin crossed with a wheat stalk inside a circle, two colors (warm brown and cream), clean lines, centered on a white background, no gradients. Negative prompt: photo realism, extra text, busy details.

Questions

What is a negative prompt?

A list of things you do not want, such as text, watermarks, or distorted hands. Some image tools have a separate field for it.

Do these work in ChatGPT and Gemini?

Yes. The structure works in chat models that make images and in dedicated image tools.
