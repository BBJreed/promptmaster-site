What you get free, and what Pro unlocks

The score is free. The finished prompt is Pro. Google Play shows the price on the purchase sheet before you confirm.

Updated October 26, 2026

Free

  • Five grades.
  • The score and a short explanation of the flaws.
  • Prompt of the day, which you can copy without spending a grade.
  • Job starters that drop a rough prompt into the box.
  • About 30 ready prompts, saved prompts, and history.
  • An optional short ad for three more grades after the five run out.

Pro

  • The full rewritten prompt.
  • Copy, and send to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or the share sheet.
  • Unlimited grades and no ads.
  • Alternate versions and the structure notes on the result.

You do not need an API key to grade a prompt. A key stays optional, and it is only used so the app can run the answer on your account. Keys stay on the device.

If the Continue button is opened in a browser, purchases are completed in the Android app. The Play sheet is the price.
