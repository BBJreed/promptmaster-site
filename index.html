Questions

What is PromptMaster?

An Android prompt optimizer and grader. Paste a prompt you wrote, get a score and what is missing, and with Pro an improved version to send to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Is it free?

Free to install. The first five grades are free, with more after a short ad. Pro unlocks the rewrite, sending, unlimited grades, and no ads.

Do I need an API key?

No. Grading works without one. You can add your own Gemini key if you want Gemini to grade; it stays on your phone.

Does it work with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini?

Yes. ChatGPT and Claude open with the rewrite filled in. Gemini gets a copy, because it does not accept a prefilled link.
